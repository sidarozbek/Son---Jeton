## 1. Logline

The stamp tallies the bankers' collapse. Sidar claims he told them so, though he didn't, and when Vehbi starts to object, a ringing payphone rescues Sidar and the fourth token goes in.

## 2. Purpose

- **Topic:** The bankers' crisis of 1981–82 and its cost (D5).
- **Tone:** The series rule holds: the people who lost their savings are never the joke.
  - The queue stays still and quiet, as in Scene 13.
  - The only joke is Sidar's: he rewrites his own part, and the phone saves him from being corrected.
- **What it does for the story:** The fourth token, the way out of the banker era and on to the harbour.

## 3. Synopsis

The office after the fall: the shutter is down, the board is dark, the sign lies on the floor, and the queue still stands there. The stamp comes down. The clipping, headlined "BANKERLER BATTI", reads: "1981–1982'de banker sistemi çöktü. Bankerlerin çoğu battı; yüz binlerce kişi birikimini kaybetti." It holds, then the office returns. Sidar, quiet at first, then talking himself into it: "I told you so." Vehbi starts: "You said you built—" A payphone on the left wall rings over him, and the line crackles. Sidar, fast and relieved: "Phone's ringing. Come." He goes round the front of the queue to the phone, Vehbi follows, and Sidar drops the fourth token in.
