## Recording conventions

- **File names:** One file per line, named `<speaker><line number>.wav`, the same as Scenes 01–03. The line number counts within the scene, so S04 starts again at 1.
  - `s` = Sidar, `v` = Vehbi, `f` = friend (S24), `p` = proctor (S23).
- **Delivery:** Send the files to the scene you are making at that time; they go into `Sxx/project/voices/raw/` as received.
- **Silences:** A silence before or after a take is fine; it gets trimmed.
- **Stamps:** "Sınavda çıkar" stamps (D1–D8) are on-screen text only and are not voiced. They stay readable for 2–3 s, so no narrator voice is needed.
- **Canon checks:**
  - Vehbi never says "I don't know" before S24.
  - Sidar never says "I was wrong".
  - No real person is named in dialogue (names appear only on stamps).
  - No line jokes about the coup or about people losing their savings.
