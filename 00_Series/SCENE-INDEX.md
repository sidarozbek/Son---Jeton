## Story plan

The canon for all 24 scenes is the person's doc “Son Jeton: 8 Dakikalık Hikâye Planı” (claude.ai/artifact/7N8ufaxBd2A7xtt7Fwv2sU): the premise, the characters, the world rules, the 24-scene table and the eight “Sınavda çıkar” stamps (D1–D8) with their sources. Each scene document quotes its own row verbatim.

Key world rules from the plan:
- Everything Sidar tells or shows is pure fantasy, and the magic never breaks.
- Only the “Sınavda çıkar” stamp gives documented information; when it lands, the picture briefly becomes a newspaper clipping.
- Fantastic events changed the world, but never any fact that answers an exam question.
- Sidar has five tokens (jeton), one crossing each; the fifth is lost, and a sixth is found in 1989.
- Real people are named only on a stamp. Fictional characters live the fantastic events.
- The coup and people losing their savings are never joked about.

Later decisions that override the plan (see S01's decisions log): the lines are spoken in English, Sidar and Vehbi are the same height, the looks follow the reference photos, and S01's “GELDİM” note was cut.
