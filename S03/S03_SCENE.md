## 1. Logline

Claiming the phone line is broken, Sidar walks Vehbi down to the basement payphone, puts the receiver in his hand and drops the first token. The line crosses, and the world folds like a sheet of paper.

## 2. Purpose

- **Topic:** The trap that starts the trip. This is the first fantastic event in the series, and there is no stamp.
- **What this scene tests:**
  - Two locations in one world, joined by a cut.
  - Props drawn live in 3D: the receiver, its coiled cord and the token.
  - The first whole-frame effect: the paper fold.
- **Comic engine:** Sidar's lie is delivered with total authority. Vehbi's answer is perfectly right ("I don't have a phone") and changes nothing. The trap works because Vehbi does what he's told.

## 3. Synopsis

Straight on from Scene 02. Sidar turns from the door and pronounces, like a repairman: "Your phone line is broken." Vehbi pushes his glasses up: "I don't have a phone." Sidar acts as if he hadn't heard: "Downstairs. Come." He is out of the door before the line ends, and Vehbi follows, book in hand.

The basement has concrete walls, a boiler, pipes, wooden stairs and one bare bulb swinging on its flex. They come off the stairs and cross to a green wall payphone. Sidar lifts the receiver and holds it out: "It's for you." Vehbi takes it to his ear. Sidar shows a brass token and drops it into the slot (an insert shot). Vehbi: "Hello?" The line hisses, the bulb stutters, and Sidar smiles for the first time in a way that means something. Then the whole picture folds like paper, in half and then in quarters, down to a small folded sheet on white.

## 7. Location and set

- **Room:** Scene 02's end state: the door at 1.72 rad, the stack at (3.29, 3.08), and the contract lying where it fell at (2.64, 3.30). The room definition itself is unchanged (`project/setdef_room.py`).
- **Basement:** x 20–25, y 0–5, 2.4 m high, in the same world beside the house, so the cut between the two is a cut, not a walk.
  - Concrete walls with a damp tide-mark and flaking whitewash; a stained concrete floor.
  - Pipes under the ceiling down to an iron boiler; a coal hatch.
  - Cardboard boxes; a shelf of jars.
  - Wooden stairs down the right wall (11 steps) with a handrail.
- **The payphone:** An original design, 0.34 × 0.52 m, on the back wall at x 22.55, bottom edge 1.05 m. It has:
  - a dark green metal case and a chrome face
  - a rotary dial
  - a coin slot top right and a coin return bottom right
  - a blank instruction card (no text, no logo, no real brand)
  - the receiver cradle on the left
- **Live props (drawn in 3D in `env.py`):**
  - **Receiver:** Its position follows the cradle, then Sidar's hand, then Vehbi's hand at his ear.
  - **Cord:** Coiled, sagging from the port under the box.
  - **Token:** Brass, with a hole.
- **Light:**
  - **Bulb:** One bare bulb at (22.35, 3.40, 2.10), swinging and settling, with a warm pool reaching about 2.5 m; ambient light elsewhere is 0.30.
  - **Flicker:** From 12.60 it stutters and keeps stuttering into the fold.
  - **Characters:** Lit as one body at their own position, as in Scenes 01–02.
- **The fold:** A whole-frame effect at the end of `env.post`, not a 3D effect. The folds go away from the viewer, so the picture stays face up until the last sheet shows its back. The void is paper-white (236, 230, 216).
- **Textures:** Painted with code in `project/make_tex_basement.py` (concrete wall and floor, stair wood, payphone, boiler, boxes). The room's textures come from Scene 01.

## 12. Known weak points and next steps

| # | Weak point | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The receiver, cord and token are drawn over the characters, not depth-sorted. It reads at these angles, but a head turning in front of the receiver would not hide it. | Keep cameras in front of the props, or sort them in a later pass. |
| 2 | Sidar's face is hidden for most of "Downstairs. Come." (by design: he is already leaving). | If the person wants his face, delay the walk by half a second. |
| 3 | The fold is a 2D effect on the finished picture; the 3D set doesn't really bend. | Acceptable as a stylised transition; a 3D fold would need a different engine feature. |
| 4 | Hands without thumbs rest on the receiver and token rather than gripping them. | A grip pass in `cast.py`. |
| 5 | Effects are synthesized; nobody here can hear the mix. | The person checks the balance. |
| 6 | Sidar's back is to the camera in the wide fold shot (H), and shot E opens on the back of his head while he lifts the receiver, so his smug smile at the crossed line is never seen. | Move camera H toward the stairs side, or add a short Sidar close-up at 12.6 s. |
