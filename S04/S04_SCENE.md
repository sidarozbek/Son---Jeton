## 1. Logline

The folded sheet opens onto a snowy street in January 1980. Sidar rattles his four remaining tokens and needles Vehbi about his exam, but Vehbi reads the date off a calendar in the grocer's window without missing a beat.

## 2. Purpose

- **Topic:** The arrival. The audience learns where and when they are, and that Sidar's tokens are counted.
- **What this scene tests:**
  - A new outdoor location: a whole street with snow, light from a shop window and a street lamp, and dusk air.
  - An insert that has to be readable (the calendar).
- **Comic engine:** Sidar sets up a trap question ("Which year?") and Vehbi answers it perfectly, which leaves Sidar nothing but "Lucky guess." Vehbi never says "I don't know".

## 3. Synopsis

The small folded sheet that ended Scene 03 opens out into a street at dusk with snow falling. Vehbi still has his hand up at his ear, holding a receiver that isn't there; he notices and lowers it. Sidar holds up four brass tokens and shakes them like dice: "Four tokens left. Don't make me waste them." Vehbi checks his watch: "My exam starts at nine." Sidar, enjoying himself: "Nine o'clock. Which year?" Vehbi's eyes flick to the grocer's window, where a tear-off calendar reads OCAK 24 1980 PERŞEMBE (an insert). He pushes up his glasses: "January twenty-fourth, nineteen eighty." Sidar mutters "…Lucky guess" and turns away to the street.

## 7. Location and set

- **Layout:**
  - **Road:** Cobbles along x, from y 0 to 4.2, with a kerb and a line of snow.
  - **Sidewalk:** Concrete slabs from y 4.2 to 6.0, with snow in the cracks and along the edges.
  - **Facades:** Along y = 6.0. The ground-floor walls are plaster over a grey base; the upper floors are ochre apartment blocks with tall windows, some lit.
- **The grocer** (x 4.4–7.6), an original shop front:
  - Painted green wood, with a blue sign board reading BAKKAL (the plain Turkish word, no brand).
  - A lit window with shelves of goods and a glass door.
  - A red-and-cream striped awning with snow on it.
  - Crates of oranges and a sack outside.
- **The calendar:** A tear-off page reading OCAK / 24 / 1980 / PERŞEMBE, hanging inside the window at x 4.66–5.00, 1.28–1.72 m up. 24 January 1980 was a Thursday.
- **Further along:** A street lamp at x 2.3 with its arm over the road; a second lamp; an original boxy van with no make; a bare tree.
- **Light** (applied over the finished picture in `env.py`):
  - **Air:** Cold blue dusk everywhere (gain about 0.40, 0.48, 0.74).
  - **Shop window:** A warm pool spilling from it over the sidewalk where they stand.
  - **Street lamp:** A softer warm pool under it.
  - **Characters:** Lit as one body at their own position, as in Scenes 01–03.
- **Snow:** Light flakes drawn over the picture.
- **The opening:** Scene 03's paper fold played backwards (`paper_fold(out, 1 - u)`), so the cut between the scenes is the same folded sheet.
- **Textures:** Painted with code in `project/make_tex_street.py` (facade, ground wall, shop front, calendar, cobbles, pavement).
