## 1. Logline

The price cards burst out of the shelves and up through the floor. Sidar and Vehbi fight their way to the shop's payphone; a flying price card sticks to Sidar's nose as he drops the second token, and the second stamp names the man behind the January decisions.

## 2. Purpose

- **Topic:** The second documented fact (D2). The 24 January decisions lifted price controls and opened the economy outward, towards export-led growth. They were prepared by Turgut Özal, the Prime Ministry undersecretary.
- **What this scene tests:**
  - A chase across a crowded set.
  - Set pieces that hide the characters.
  - A prop that lands on a character's face.
- **Comic engine:**
  - Sidar's urgency.
  - Vehbi's politeness to cardboard.
  - Sidar staying dignified with a price on his nose.

## 3. Synopsis

The swollen price cards keep swelling, and three giant cards (240, 300, 150) shoot up out of the floor. The grocer is squeezed flat behind his counter. Sidar points: "To the phone! Run!" He runs round the right of the cards to a payphone on the shop's right wall. Vehbi weaves between the giant cards, nodding politely to each: "Excuse me. Excuse me. Excuse me." A small price card flies off the shelf and slaps onto Sidar's nose. He turns round, card on his face, and says, muffled: "Token two." He reaches behind him, blind, and drops the token in the slot. The line crosses; the stamp comes down. In the clipping, under the headline "İHRACATA DAYALI BÜYÜME", the body reads: "24 Ocak kararlarıyla fiyat kontrolleri kaldırıldı, ekonomi dışa açıldı. Hazırlayan: Başbakanlık Müsteşarı Turgut Özal." It holds to the end.

## 7. Location and set

- **The shop:** As Scene 07 left it, with three additions:
  - **The payphone:** On the right wall near the front, at (4.94, 1.10), 1.05 m up, facing into the shop. It is Scene 03's original green design, with no logo.
  - **Three giant standing cards:** 240, 300 and 150, each 0.7 × 0.9 m, the same hand-lettered style as the small cards. They shoot up out of the floor at (2.35, 1.95), (3.00, 1.30) and (2.05, 0.85), with a small bounce.
  - **The flying card:** A small "45" card flies from the shelf in an arc, tumbling, while it is a Blender card. Once it lands (5.85), `env.draw_nose_card` draws it flat on Sidar's face, over the character, so his own head can't hide it.
- **Hiding behind the set:** Every character is now cut wherever the set is nearer to the camera than they are (`env._behind_counter`, applied to all three), so the giant cards hide Vehbi as he weaves behind them.
- **The stamp and the clipping:** Drawn by `stamp.py`, shared with Scene 07. Long headlines and body lines now shrink to fit the page.
