### SIDAR: *The Landlord Who Won't Be Stopped* (locked)

**Visual:** Short and wide (narrower than the stocky preset, at the person's request), head about one third of the body, barrel chest and belly, short thick arms, hands with four broad fingers and no thumb. Big round full face with heavy cheeks; small dark eyes behind thick, dark, rectangular glasses (frame #1E1E22, bold graphic shape); heavy straight brows that sit low. Thick, full grey hair, short and dense, combed back (#8E8E8A). Full grey beard, a little longer than a trim, ending just below the chin in one solid mass so the tie shows beneath it (#9C9C98); a thin, long, darker grey moustache over it whose ends sweep out past the mouth corners and droop (#6E6E6A); the mouth opening stays visible under the moustache for lip-sync. Warm tan skin (#C49A7A). Grey shirt (#8A8E92), brown vest stretched over the belly (#6A4A32), burgundy tie as the signature accent (#6E2430), dark brown slippers (#4A3426). Default expression: brows down, jaw set, glare. Never thin, never clean-shaven, never thinning or bald, never a white or short-cropped beard, never a bushy moustache, never round glasses, never an embarrassed or sheepish face.

**Function in the story:** The chaos. A rental contract tries to hold him back; he turns its own wording into the way in. The rent is the excuse; loneliness is the reason, and he will never say so.

**Backstory:** Retired central bank clerk, about 70. He knows paperwork inside out, so he follows the rules to the letter whenever that gets him what he wants. Formative detail: since retiring he still ties his tie every morning, with nowhere to go.

**Present-tense psychology:** Night, alone in the building. He pounds on the tenant's door over the rent, and both of them know that isn't what it's about. Being out-argued only makes him push harder.

**Speech:** "deep, loud, chesty voice with a gravelly edge, short barked bursts, commanding volume, plain old-office vocabulary, speaks in declarations not questions, never admits a mistake"

**Voice source:** a cloned voice of a consenting person [TBD: which one, recorded later]. Spoken language: English.

**Sample lines:** "Vehbi! Rent!" · "I'm giving notice!" · "I'm here."

**Movement:** "sudden moves led by the shoulder: the shoulder arrives first and the body follows, short heavy shuffling steps in slippers, snaps into big held poses, belly, beard and tie overlap and settle after each stop, leans in to win an argument, never steps back"

**Stillness:** "wide planted stance with weight on the heels, chest out, fists low at the sides or one hand on the hip, slow heavy breath through the nose that lifts the beard, eyes fixed and unblinking behind thick lenses, no fidgeting"

**Comic mechanics:** Takes every rule literally and turns it into his weapon (asked for notice, he delivers himself: "I'm here."). When he loses, he doubles down instead of conceding. Signature reaction: a slow shoulder-first lean into the other person's space. Running gag: in every argument he pulls a document from his pocket, and each one is more absurd than the last.

**Engine limits:** body preset stocky; default face is `rest` with `lid=.28, brow=-.25` (a glare, never wide-eyed at rest); face limit: no embarrassed or sheepish expression; one beat of stillness before every sudden move.

**Asset tags:** head lock (front, 3/4, profile, back), full-body model sheet.

### VEHBI: *The Clause Reader* (locked)

**Visual:** Short and thin, exactly Sidar's height, head about one quarter of the body, narrow shoulders, slim arms and legs, slim hands with four fingers and no thumb. Long oval face with a narrow chin; calm, slightly heavy-lidded dark eyes behind thin, round glasses (dark frame #2A2A2E, thin graphic line); straight, level brows. Dark, straight, shoulder-length hair with a straight cut at the ends, framing the face on both sides and falling to the shoulders at the back (#1E1A18); never curly or wavy. A thin dark moustache set high, just under the nose, whose ends run down the mouth corners into a short dark goatee, joined to a thin beard line along the jaw; clean cheeks (#24201C). The moustache lifts with the upper lip and the goatee drops with the jaw when he speaks. Fair warm skin (#E0BC9E). Petrol green sweater as the signature colour (#1F5A5C), dark grey trousers (#2E3138), grey socks, no shoes. Always holds a thick economics textbook with an ochre cover (#B8862E); the rental contract is tucked inside it. Default expression: neutral, mouth closed, faintly unimpressed. Never tall, never chubby, never clean-shaven, never a full beard, never a thick moustache, never rectangular glasses, never without the book, never a panicked face.

**Function in the story:** The one who knows. He believes paper protects him: a clause for everything, read out calmly. The joke is that he is always right, and it never helps.

**Backstory:** 22, economics student. Exam tomorrow. Formative detail: he keeps every document in the textbook he is studying from, so the book is also his filing cabinet.

**Present-tense psychology:** The night before the exam, studying at the desk. He hears the pounding and doesn't bother to get up at first. He is sure the contract will settle it, and doesn't yet know the contract will be used against him.

**Speech:** "flat, confident mid-low voice, even volume that never rises, clean dry tone, precise complete sentences, quotes rules by number, short pause before the decisive word, never says 'I don't know'"

**Voice source:** a cloned voice of a consenting person [TBD: which one, recorded later]. Spoken language: English. Must sound clearly different from Sidar's voice.

**Sample lines:** "Clause seven: the landlord gives notice before coming at night." · "In writing."

**Movement:** "stiff, measured moves, one joint at a time, small exact gestures, turns the head before the body, adjusts the glasses with one finger before speaking, slow deliberate steps, the book never leaves one hand"

**Stillness:** "upright and narrow, weight evenly on both feet or seated very straight, the book held against the chest or open on one forearm, eyes on the page, a slow blink, almost no breath movement"

**Comic mechanics:** The straight man: precise, correct and powerless. The calmer he stays, the funnier the loss. Signature reaction: a slow look down at the paper he has just been handed, then a slow look up. Running gag: he pushes his glasses up before every rule he quotes.

**Engine limits:** thin build at the same height as the stocky preset (match head height in the turnaround); hand limit: one hand always holds the book; face limit: no panicked expression (jaw held almost shut, eye size and brow lift capped); brows move little.

**Asset tags:** head lock (front, 3/4, profile, back), full-body model sheet, the textbook with the contract inside as a prop.
