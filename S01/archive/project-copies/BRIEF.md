- **Topic / what it teaches:** A rental contract cannot stop a lonely, furious landlord.
- **Audience and spoken language:** University students with short attention spans. Spoken lines in English (the person's decision; the original Turkish lines are kept below for reference).
- **Length of this test:** About 20 seconds, flexible. The person said the scene matters more than the number: let it run longer if the timing needs it (keep one scene file under about 30 seconds, per PIPELINE).
- **Characters:**
  - **Vehbi** (tenant): 22, economics student.
    - Silhouette: short and thin, the same height as Sidar; messy dark hair. (Changed from "tall" by the person.)
    - Palette: petrol green sweater.
    - Never: says "bilmiyorum"; lets go of his book (enforce as a rig limit: one hand always holds the book).
    - Moves: stiff and measured.
    - Sounds: flat and confident.
  - **Sidar** (landlord): about 70, retired central bank clerk.
    - Silhouette: short and wide, thick glasses, grey moustache.
    - Palette: grey shirt, brown vest, tie, slippers.
    - Never: says "yanılmışım"; looks embarrassed (enforce as a face limit: no embarrassed or sheepish expression).
    - Moves: sudden, led by the shoulder.
    - Sounds: deep and loud.
- **Place and period:** A small rented room at night, invented. Desk lamp, stacks of books, and a door to a dim corridor. Period: present day (assumed). Nothing real to verify.
