## 1. Logline

The night before his exam, a student reads his landlord the clause that forbids night visits without notice. The landlord delivers the notice in person, through the door.

## 2. Purpose

- **Topic:** A rental contract cannot stop a lonely, furious landlord.
- **Audience:** University students with short attention spans.
- **What this scene tests:** The comic timing of spoken lines, and whether the two characters read clearly side by side.
- **Comic engine:** Vehbi is right and it never helps. Sidar takes every rule literally and turns it into his way in: asked for notice, he delivers himself.

## 3. Synopsis

Vehbi studies at his desk under the lamp. Someone pounds on the door; the lamp jumps with each knock, and Vehbi doesn't look up. In the corridor, Sidar shouts for the rent. Vehbi pushes his glasses up, walks to the door, opens it a crack, and reads clause seven from the contract he keeps inside his textbook: the landlord gives notice before coming at night. Sidar says he is giving notice. Vehbi says it must be in writing. After a beat, Sidar shoulders the door wide open. The door slams against its stop, a stack of books skids across the floor, and Sidar walks in. "I'm here." Vehbi gives him a long, slow look. The camera cranes up: two small people in a lamp-lit room.

## 7. Location and set

- **Description:** A small rented room at night. Pale mint distemper above an oil-painted dado and a dark skirting board, on a patterned vinyl floor (muşamba). On the left wall, a window with a net curtain onto a night street. At the desk under the window: a green desk lamp with an amber shade, book stacks, a tulip tea glass, the printed contract form with its revenue stamp, and photocopied notes. A chair, a bed with a burgundy wool blanket, and a panel radiator by the door. A four-panel painted door opens onto a cold blue-grey corridor with terrazzo tiles, an unlit fixture and two neighbours' doors.
- **Size:**
  - Room: 4.0 × 4.4 × 2.7 m. x runs from the desk wall (0) to the bed wall (4.0); y runs from the front wall (0) to the door wall (4.4).
  - Door opening: x 2.60–3.50, height 2.05 m, hinged on the bed side at (3.50, 4.40), swinging into the room.
  - Corridor: y 4.4–6.0, x −2.5 to 7.5.
- **Cultural layer:**
  - A stationery-shop rental contract form with a revenue stamp
  - Photocopied lecture notes
  - A tulip tea glass
  - A panel radiator
  - A net curtain
  - Patterned vinyl flooring
- **Light:**
  - **Lamp:** One warm desk-lamp pool, applied in 3D to the finished picture from the depth buffer and centred under the shade at (0.30, 1.44, 1.16). It reaches about 2.3 m; ambient light elsewhere in the room is 0.36.
  - **Corridor:** Cold and dim, with warm spill through the door in proportion to how far it is open.
  - **Characters:** Each is lit as one body by the light at its own chest.
- **Shadows and grade:**
  - **Shadows:** Short (`TO_LIGHT = (-.40, -.42, .81)`, `SHADOW = .40`), so neither character's shadow falls across the other.
  - **Grade:** Saturation 0.90, a warm tint, a soft vignette and dust in the air.
- **Textures:** Painted with code (`project/make_tex.py`). The cut-out props are the door, lamp, book stack, tea glass and radiator.

## 15. How to re-render

From a Toon Studio folder with the engine set up (`tools/setup.sh`):

```
python3 engine/toon.py <path>/S01/project lint
python3 engine/toon.py <path>/S01/project animatic     # timing, about 6 min
python3 engine/toon.py <path>/S01/project final        # full render, 35 to 45 min on 2 cores
```

- **What gets redone:** Only the plates whose camera or set changed are re-rendered.
- **Sound only:** Run `mix`, then mux the video with ffmpeg.
