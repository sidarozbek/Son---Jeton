## 1. Logline

Sidar claims he was "in that room" in January 1980. Vehbi's textbook disagrees, so Sidar fights the book, and loses to a student who never lets go.

## 2. Purpose

- **Topic:** Sidar's claim to history, with no fact on screen yet. There is no stamp in this scene.
- **Audience:** University students with short attention spans.
- **What this scene tests:**
  - Two characters physically connected through one prop: moving hand pins on a book that travels between them.
  - A paper prop falling in 3D.
- **Comic engine:** Sidar's pride against the book, which neither of them will let go of. Sidar never says he was wrong ("Then the book is wrong!") and never looks embarrassed (he straightens his tie as if he had won).

## 3. Synopsis

Straight on from Scene 01. Sidar puffs up and tells Vehbi he was in that room in January 1980. He points up and narrows his eyes as if he can see it. Vehbi pushes his glasses up, opens his textbook and turns it to him: "That's not what it says." Sidar reads, slowly, and the anger arrives late. "Then the book is wrong!" He lunges and seizes the book. A tug of war follows: Sidar yanks five times, throwing his whole weight back, while Vehbi barely sways and never lets go. The two turn half a circle around the book and swap places. The rental contract slips out from between the pages and drifts to the floor. Sidar lets go all at once and straightens his tie, as if nothing had happened. Vehbi looks down at the contract, then slowly back up at him. Sidar, not looking at Vehbi, surveys the room, and his eyes go to the open door.
